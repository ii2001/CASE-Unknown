# Product requirements

CASE: UNKNOWN is a Korean-first, single-player generative detective game. A case is fixed at creation; play consists of moving, inspecting, interviewing, reviewing and making one final accusation. Normal cases expose three suspects, four locations and six-to-eight clues. Success is exact culprit selection; reasoning is recorded but never lets an LLM override the verdict. Mock mode is the acceptance baseline and requires no credentials.

Replayability is supplied by a fresh case identifier and selectable genre. The MVP ships one fully authored solvable scenario beneath all mock genres; true generative variety is the first post-MVP production task.
